# Explanation

Diátaxis section: **understanding-oriented**.

Explanation pages give context. They tell the reader why the system is the
way it is, what tradeoffs were made, and how the parts fit together.
They are not how-tos and they are not reference.

## Conventions

- Discursive voice; whole paragraphs are fine
- Compare alternatives and explain rejection rationale
- Link out to ADRs for the canonical record of any specific decision
- Don't include step-by-step instructions or tables of options
